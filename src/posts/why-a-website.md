The open web, as we know it, is in a strange place right now. The major tech players on the field, those ones that profited handsomely on top of it, now have declared an [open war](https://tante.cc/2026/05/20/on-google-declaring-war-on-the-web/) on the very openess of the web, the one that allowed them to thrive in the first place.

A major part of the web nowadays it noise, generated by the sausage content machines that are being hyped as 'intelligent machines'.

## Owning my web

In the other side, I think that this kind of climate poses us a question: how to reclaim the web back? There's a steady movement of personal websites happening for some time now. I find this very exciting, to renew our hopes on a web that we can be active participants on it, and not mere consumers of 'content'.

## What I'm planning to do

I really don't know. My initial idea is to make this website like a personal notebook, where I talk out loud with myself, in public. Or a digital garden (maybe both). Publish some of my projects, try to write more technical articles, things like that.

Let's start.
